Research

I am a PhD researcher at Technische Universität Berlin and BIFOLD working on AI safety, chain-of-thought monitoring, reinforcement learning, and sustainable computing. My recent research investigates how internal activations and reasoning dynamics can be used to detect, predict, and control reward hacking and risky behavior in language-model agents.

01

Reasoning oversight

Chain-of-Thought Monitoring, reasoning dynamics, and methods for detecting when models overthink.

02

Mechanistic monitoring

Internal, representation-level signals for understanding and supervising capable LLM agents.

03

RL post-training

Reinforcement Learning for language models, scaling, compute allocation and reward signals.

Efficient AI systems

Sustainable systems and infrastructure for resource-aware machine learning research and deployment.

What I work on

Across these projects, I combine empirical studies of model behavior with tools for inspecting model internals and the systems work needed to run experiments efficiently. I am especially interested in research that turns safety concepts into practical evaluations and monitoring methods.
